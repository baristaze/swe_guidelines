Privacy

Acme is a multi-tenant system for teams: its site and its app. This page says what it keeps about you, who else receives any of it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to privacy@acme.example.

What Acme keeps

  • Your account: your email address and name, as the sign-in provider confirms them, and the orgs you belong to with your role in each.
  • Your work: what you and your team create in it, the files you attach, and a record of who changed what and when.
  • Service records: each request's time, route, and status, with no request body and no query string, and reports of the app's own errors, written so that no text a person wrote goes in them.

The company site sets no cookie and runs no script. The app keeps its sign-in in your browser's session storage and your display preferences in its local storage, and sets no tracking cookie. Acme shows no ads and sells no data.

Who else receives some of it

Acme uses these services, each for one purpose and each receiving only what that purpose needs:

  • Amazon Web Services (United States, us-west-2) runs the app and stores its data, encrypted at rest.
  • WorkOS signs you in: it receives your email address, sends the sign-in code, and holds your sign-in identity.
  • Sentry receives reports of the app's errors, which carry a request id and no text a person wrote.

Acme shares nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and your work stay while your account and your org exist.
  • The record of changes is kept for 90 days.
  • Service records are kept for 30 days.
  • A deleted item leaves the database within 30 days, and its backups within 7 days after that.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app's Settings. The deletion is at once and final: your account, your personal org with its records and files, and your sign-in identity at WorkOS go. The work you did in a team org stays the team's. An org's owner can delete a team org the same way. Everything deleted leaves the backups within 7 days. You can also ask for a copy or a deletion at privacy@acme.example.
