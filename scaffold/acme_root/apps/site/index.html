The to-do list your team shares, live.

Acme is a to-do app for teams. Add a task, check one off, or move it to the top, and everyone on the team sees it the moment it happens. No refresh, no sync button. Everything the app does, the API does too.

What it does today

Small on purpose. Fast everywhere.

One list per team, one per person, and everything around it that a team actually uses.

  • A realtime task list

    Adds, edits, check-offs, and reorders reach every open tab of the team at once. A dropped connection catches up on its own, and nothing is lost. Select a few tasks, or all of them, and mark them done in one go; Undo takes it back.

  • Teams, and a list of your own

    Everyone gets a personal org for their own tasks. Start a team org when you need one, invite people, and switch between them in one click.

  • Reminders

    Give a task a due date and Acme reminds the team that morning: on every open screen, and in Slack when a channel is connected.

  • Slack, both ways

    Add Acme to your Slack workspace and the team's changes are posted in the channel you pick. Type /acme for your open tasks, /acme team for the team's, or /acme add to add one without leaving the conversation.

  • Files on tasks

    Attach a file to a task: the spec, the photo, the receipt. Each one shows a preview right on the task.

  • Your list, in and out

    Import the list you have from a CSV file. Done tasks nobody touched for ninety days move to an archive, one click from coming back. Delete your account from Settings and it is gone.

  • An API, a Python client, a CLI

    Make an API key and script the list, or let an agent work it. The Python client and the acme command line use the same API, and acme listen streams every change.

  • Light and dark

    Acme follows your system's light or dark theme, or the one you pick in the app.
